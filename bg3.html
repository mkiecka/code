In the hit video game Baldur's Gate 3, the concept of all-consuming love is depicted through a variety of its characters and narratives. Whether it be through its depiction of vampire spawns as subservient vassals to the true vampires that turned them, or the blind devotion through the universes Gods, there is no absence of agape love. Still, there is no better example that illustrates the depths of steadfast, gruesome dedication than that of the Bhallspawn. The mortal offspring of the world's God of murder, said to experience visions of bloodshed and terror, and acting on murder in their unconscious.

One of said spawn is Orin the Red, a changeling who wishes to claim the title of the single chosen of Bhaal through any means necessary. Her identity is entirely intertwined with her God to the extent that she is hardly ever truly herself, constantly depicting herself through various identities. Her acts of violence are nothing if not a passionate act of devotion, twisting, crushing, and torturing her victims while revelling at the mere thought. Murder and defilement of her victims is pure, hedonistic pleasure to her. It's an indulgence in the basest of desires, a true severing of the restraints imposed by conventional morality. A willful return to the naturalistic brutality of the simplest of creatures.
