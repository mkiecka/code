we focus on the perspective of the “giver” of flesh, and how thrilling that can be. Bella was always a nobody, a drifter, going from one home to another and never making lasting connections - her parents weren't particularly invested in her and neither were her peers. She desperately needed someone to desire her presence, to desire her. And here comes a handsome stranger, who is instantly infatuated with her, who wants her right to the bone. “You are my own personal brand of heroin”, as Edward himself put it. He can't trust himself around her, that's how intense the hunger is. It's never happened with anyone else and presumably never would happen again. Bella is finally special to someone, she feels like she can provide something no one else could. And that excites her. To finally hold any semblance of power over someone else or even just her own life.

It's exhilarating to be wanted so deeply, so intensely ,that even someone so far removed from your world would want to throw everything away just to have you. The desire is carnal in its basest way, and that couldn't make her happier - to have the power to reduce a composed man to a beast.
