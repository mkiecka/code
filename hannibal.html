Hannibal is one of the many reimaginings and spin-off stories based on the novel series by Thomas Harris, sitting amongst such classics as the 1991 film Silence of the Lambs - even then, it manages to stand out as a simply outstanding piece of media, and undisputedly the one that has affected me (and many others) the most: not just compared to the rest of the franchise but amongst media in general.

To anyone familiar with the name “Hannibal Lecter” it's clear why the show is on this list. “He eats people. And maybe there's some love in there, I don't know.” Oh but there's more. So much more.

No matter what I say about the show, it's going to be an understatement. It's impossible to not undersell it (in my opinion at least). The themes, the characters, the imagery, the Everything - it's just excellent. No show has affected me like this one has and I don't think any ever will. If you like seeing bloody men (almost) kissing and homoerotic murder, this one's for you.

For now I'll just leave you with a quote that encompasses the theme of consumption metaphors for love:

“Is Hannibal in love with me?”

“Could he daily feel a stab of hunger and find nourishment at the very sight of you? Yes. But do you ache for him?”
