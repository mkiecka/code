Wanting to be loved and known is one of the most fundamental human feelings. Wanting to be close to someone, figuratively and literally. Sometimes a little TOO close, perhaps. Yes, people sometimes say they love someone so much they want to eat them up. But they can't mean it literally … right? Well, it turns out there is a very thin line between the romantic and the macabre to some, and the idea of all-consuming love is taken to new heights. To want and be wanted right down to the bone. In a fascinating meeting of romance and horror, sometimes creators choose to explore that particular metaphor in a more literal (and bloody) way.

Below are selected examples of media with interesting takes on this subject. Browse away!
