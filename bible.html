Even when removed from its religious connotations, the Christian Bible is a fascinating piece of text. Considering how many themes and topics it touches upon, eating people was bound to come up. This isn't about some obscure Old Testament story, though; a much more recent (and more widely known, though not necessarily talked about in this context) element of the text with cannibalistic tones is one of the holy sacraments itself: the Holy Communion. It is, in the most literal sense, a consumption of flesh as a show of devotion and commitment on the part of the believers, and a sharing of oneself on the part of Christ.

The eating of flesh here is mostly seen here as a voluntary sacrifice, an offering of divinity to the common man; willingly giving up parts of the self to help others make themselves whole.
