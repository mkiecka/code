In her 2022 debut album, Ethel Cain (real name Hayden Anhedönia) weaves an intricate storyline through the 13 songs featured, telling tales of love, heartbreak, nostalgia and, of course, cannibalism.

Ethel, the character whose eyes we experience this world through, is the titular preacher's daughter, coming of age in rural Appalachia. As she explores the complicated feelings related to her faith and the abuse she suffers at the hands of people in her community, she finds escape in the arms of a man named Isaiah, who she meets after running away from home in search for a better life. The initial infatuation quickly turns sour, however, culminating in the 9th song of the record, “Ptolomea”.

Isaiah's desire for Ethel twists itself into something dark devious, ultimately manifesting itself as a literal craving for her flesh. Directly referencing her deep Christian faith, he tells her "You love blood too much / But not like I do” - Ethel craves the purity in the blood of Jesus, while Isaiah craves the feeling of pleasure he will gain by slaughtering Ethel, through the shedding of her blood. In the end he makes good on his work, killing and cannibalising her. Ethel, who we hear in her spectral form speaking from the great beyond, expresses no wrath at this action and instead only asking for a final confirmation that what Isaiah felt towards her was love, and expresses apologia at the thought of her beloved experiencing adverse effects from the act (“I just wanted to be yours, can I be yours? / Can I be yours? Just tell me I'm yours / If I'm turning in your stomach and I'm making you feel sick / Am I making you feel sick?” (Strangers)).
